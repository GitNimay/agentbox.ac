Conductor alternatives

Conductor runs several Claude Code agents in isolated workspaces on a Mac. It is good, and it is closed source, macOS-only, and $50 a month for Pro. Those three facts are why most people end up here.

Written 16 October 2026. Prices read from conductor.build/pricing and star counts from the GitHub API that day. We have not run every tool below for a month — where we state a fact it is a checkable one, not a verdict on how it feels.

What Conductor is, fairly

A macOS app that gives each Claude Code agent its own isolated workspace so several can work at once without colliding. It is a funded company — YC-backed, with a Series A — and it shows: the app is polished, it has cloud workspaces that run your agents off your machine, and Pro includes a mobile app.

Current pricing, from their own page today: Free $0, Pro $50/mo, Teams $60/mo per user, Enterprise custom. The free plan is described as local workspaces on your Mac.

Why people look for an alternative

From what people actually say when they ask: it is Mac-only and they are on Windows or Linux; they do not want a $50 monthly bill on top of a Claude subscription they already pay for; or they want to read the source of something that is running agents against their codebase. All three are reasonable, and only the first is a matter of taste.

When Conductor is the better choice

Often, and it would be dishonest to bury this at the bottom in small type.

  • You want agents running when your laptop is shut. Conductor has cloud workspaces. We do not — Agentbox runs on your machine, and when it sleeps, so do the agents.
  • You want to check on work from your phone. Pro includes a mobile app. We have nothing to offer you here today.
  • You are buying for a team. There is a Teams plan, seats, and a company to send an invoice. We have none of that.
  • You want support and a roadmap you can rely on. A funded company is a real answer to that. A three-week-old project with 77 stars is not.
  • You want the most polished Mac app in the category. Conductor has had far longer to make it so.

When Agentbox is the better choice

  • You do not want another monthly bill. Agentbox is free and runs on the Claude Code or Codex plan you already pay for. No API key goes anywhere near it.
  • You want to read the source. It is GPL-3.0 and it is all there. For software that runs commands against your repository, some people consider that non-negotiable.
  • You are not on a Mac. npx agentbox-app runs the whole thing in a browser on your own machine, on any platform with Node 22, listening only on 127.0.0.1. Linux installs also get a launcher entry. Conductor has no answer here at all.
  • You want no account and no server. There is nothing to sign into and nothing to send your code to.
  • You use Codex as well as Claude Code. Both are first-class, side by side.
  • The number of agents is the problem. Agentbox is built around the moment after an agent finishes: the ones that need you rise to the top, and you answer, approve or close them the way you clear email. There is a board too, which is how a lot of people actually use it.

Moving across

There is nothing to export. Both tools drive the same Claude Code on the same repositories, so switching is installing the other one and pointing it at the same folder. Agentbox makes a git worktree per task under .claude/worktrees/; any branches you already have are untouched. If you do not like it, delete the app — there is no account to close.
