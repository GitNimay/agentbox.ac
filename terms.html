What Agentbox is

Agentbox is a Mac application that works with the copy of Claude Code on your computer, and during setup it can install Claude Code or Codex for you. It shows you the agents you are running, lets you answer them, and runs everything on your own machine under your own user account. It asks you to sign in when you first open it, and you can skip that. If you sign in, it backs up your threads to your account so that you can reach them from your phone, as section 5.8 of the privacy policy describes. Unless you sign in or choose Agentbox Cloud, which section 19 covers, it stores nothing of yours on our systems and it holds no account for you.

Agents run real commands on your real machine

Agentbox drives Claude Code. Claude Code edits real files and runs real commands on your computer with the permissions you have given it. When you approve something in Agentbox it happens for real, and it can create, change and delete files, run programs, use the network and act on anything your user account can reach.

Agentbox managed Codex workers do not automatically inherit Codex apps or plugins. The local task store connection may remain available to project workers. This restriction is scoped to those workers and does not change your native Codex configuration.

A message sent during active work may be applied at the provider's next processing step. A correction does not undo actions already taken and does not change the running session's permissions. If delivery cannot be confirmed, Agentbox reports that uncertainty. Check the conversation before sending the same instruction again.

Commands you enter in the built-in terminal execute directly under your computer account. They are not agent requests and do not pass through provider approval cards or provider sandbox rules. They can change or delete files, run programs and use the network. Hiding the terminal or changing tasks leaves its shell running. Ending the session or quitting Agentbox ends its managed processes. Commands are not automatically restarted when Agentbox opens again.

Personal Claude Code conversations use the same approval cards as project tasks when the provider requests permission. Connecting this approval channel does not automatically grant permission, enable additional integrations or apply the broader permissions of a project worker. Existing provider permissions and denials still apply.

You decide what to approve and you are responsible for what follows, including what an agent does that you did not intend. Agents are not deterministic and they make mistakes. Keep your work in version control, keep backups you can restore from, and do not grant an agent access to anything you cannot afford to lose.

Remote Control is optional and must be enabled for a conversation. It connects your existing Claude Code session to your Anthropic account for access from another device. Remote messages can cause the local agent to use the tools and permissions available to that session. Keep Agentbox and the computer running while using it. Provider eligibility, sign in, connectivity and session availability can prevent or interrupt access. Turning it off disconnects the bridge without cancelling unrelated tasks. Provider retention is governed by your agreement with that provider.

We do not review, supervise or control what an agent does. Agentbox shows you what is happening and asks you before an action that needs approval, and it does not warrant that any particular action will be caught, shown or stopped.

Where you have connected a service to Claude Code, such as Slack or Linear, an agent may read from it without asking you each time, signed in as you and under your own agreement with that service. In this version an agent cannot send or post anything through a connected service. If that changes, this section will say what an agent may send without your approval before any version that can send is released.
