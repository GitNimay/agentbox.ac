How these are written

Star counts, licences, platforms and commit dates come from the GitHub API on the day of writing, not from other articles, and the comparison tables are generated from that data rather than typed by hand.

This category moves fast enough that four tools still being recommended in articles published this year are dead, deprecated or have changed into something else. Anything out of date on these pages is worth an issue, which gets it corrected.
